## I start the blog because I'm tired ...

As I near the end of my bachelor's program in computer science, I have started to think about what comes next. With technology blooming, especially large language models (LLMs), there are fewer opportunities for entry-level developers. On one hand I love technology, but on the other I also hate it. Not because it replaces me, but because it has taken away the fun of learning to code. I am tired of interacting with artificial intelligence instead of human intelligence. To save myself from an "AI-Induced Delusional Spirals", I have started writing.
